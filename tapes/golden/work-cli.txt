$ qits work list --help | grep -A 2 -E -- '--(unassigned|by-priority|limit|assigned-to-me)'
Usage: qits work list [-hV] [--assigned-to-me] [--by-priority] [--unassigned]
                      [--agents-url=<url>] [--archetype=<archetype>]
                      [--assignee=<text>] [--entity=<entity>] [--limit=<n>]
                      [-o=table|json] [--outcome=<outcome>] [--parent=<entity>]
                      [--project=<project>] [--projects-url=<url>]
--
A project's long-running agent finds its work with --unassigned --by-priority
(what it could take next, in the backlog order) and --assigned-to-me (what it
holds). Both may leave out --project: --assigned-to-me always, --unassigned
when GET /agents/api/me knows the caller; the project is then the agent's own.
      --agents-url=<url>     The agents service's base URL, without /agents,
                               which --assigned-to-me and --unassigned ask who
                               the caller is. Default: QITS_AGENTS_URL, else
                               the session's idp address with `idp` swapped for
--
      --assigned-to-me       Only the items you hold: assigned to your name and
                               not yet at an end. For a project's long-running
                               agent: the name and the default project come
--
                               --unassigned.
      --assignee=<text>      Only items assigned to this name, such as a
                               project's long-running agent's (developer). Not
                               with --unassigned or --assigned-to-me.
      --by-priority          The backlog order: bugs first, then priority
                               (highest first), then oldest.
      --entity=<entity>      The work entity: its id or its qualified id
--
      --limit=<n>            At most this many items, 1 to 200.
  -o, --output=table|json    table (the default): aligned columns. json: the
